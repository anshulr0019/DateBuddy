# Earth textures

- Creator: Solar System Scope / INOVE
- Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- earth-day.jpg: 2k_earth_daymap.jpg
- earth-clouds.jpg: 2k_earth_clouds.jpg

Original images are bundled locally, unchanged. The globe projects, lights and animates these textures at runtime.

- earth-land-mask.png: derived from 2k_earth_specular_map.tif, inverted, reduced to 1024 × 512 and softened for the cartoon coastline.
- earth-cartoon-preview.png: transparent static render of our cartoon globe using the derived land mask; used when WebGL is unavailable.
